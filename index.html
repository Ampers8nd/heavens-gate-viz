MAP NOTES

Systems, worlds
and territories.

Define factions in the workbook’s Factions sheet. Add their IDs to faction_owners in Stars or Planets, separated by semicolons: union; league.

Stars with the same system_id share the combined system claims. Planet owners are recorded separately. Blank means no ownership recorded.

2D view flattens the live star coordinates onto X/Y and separates markers that align on screen. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and click a marker or name to select a star. The faction legend uses the workbook's faction names and colors.

Export 2D downloads the currently filtered chart as a PNG. It names every star and includes scale rings and faction claim arcs. Aligned points are separated and connected to their original positions. Z is deliberately omitted.

Save workbook edits, then reload it here. Open workbook reads a local file without uploading it. The map never writes to your Excel file.

Positions are HYG J2000 equatorial coordinates in light-years. Marker sizes and territory rings are symbolic. Planet measurements and names are from the supplied NASA export.
